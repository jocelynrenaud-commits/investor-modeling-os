prototype  ·  31 Oct 2026

One household on every tab, and safer what-ifs

  • One household everywhere. A Balances tab now drives Overview, Holdings, the Equity Curve and Retirement alike (then a Holdings tab, then the sample household), and a Balances tab is no longer read as balance history.
  • What If I Add This Deal? is a preview. Run it changes nothing; Add to my Retirement plan includes the deal, with Remove beside it. Past years, impossible paid-in figures and commitments larger than your public investments are refused with a message.
  • A new book starts clean: a considered deal, filters and balances recorded this session no longer carry over to the next file.
  • Percent cells read the way you typed them: "1%", "1.2%" and 1.2 are all about one percent, never 100 times that.
  • New random streams. Every deal now has its own seeded stream, so adding a deal never reshuffles the futures of the deals you already hold. Figures moved slightly once because of it.
  • Plain words: Chance the money lasts, Still to pay in, Paid in, a Terms card on Help, a Start Here card on Overview, and dark mode.

My Ultimate Investor Modeling OS

  • Retirement now says what it includes: capital calls still owed (on by default), your tax strategies, and a deal you are considering, each with the plan with and without it.
  • What If I Add This Deal? on the Deal Library: a deal's own range and its effect on your retirement.
  • If You Invest What It Saves on Tax Strategies: the savings compounding at a rate you choose, and the break-even rate.
  • Solar investment credit joins Tax Strategies: 30% off the tax itself for projects in service by the end of 2027, depreciation, income, and the 5-year clawback.
  • Oil and gas drilling joins Tax Strategies: intangible drilling costs deducted up front, declining production taxed after depletion, working interest against limited partner.
  • The What-If tab is removed.
  • Cost segregation joins Tax Strategies: deductions moved forward, recapture at a sale, and whether you can use the loss at all.
  • Roth conversion joins Tax Strategies: tax now against tax-free withdrawals later, with the rate you pay compared to the rate you avoid. Pair it with Heavy Machinery and the deduction absorbs the conversion.

Tax Strategies, five accounts, a new look

  • Tax Strategies tab. Pick a strategy, set its terms, see the tax saved and owed back year by year. Heavy Machinery and Workforce Housing to start; more plug in. Send the plan to What-If in one click.
  • Five accounts: IRA and Self-Directed IRA (pre-tax), Roth IRA, Private Deals and Brokerage (taxable).
  • Equity Curve: a new plan every Jan 1, from the Dec 31 balance.
  • Hover any chart for the numbers behind it.
prototype  ·  28 Oct 2026

One tool instead of five

Built on the Financial Planning OS (v1.50): the private-deal dashboard, the liquidity outlook and the Retirement Simulator are unchanged.

New: the What-If tab. Your plan beside a few changes to it, run through the same simulation with the same random draws so only the change differs. It models:

  • Tax plays, once or year after year: Workforce Housing and Heavy Machinery, using a verified port of the two published federal tax models (6,145 checks, matching to the cent). Stacked plays are measured against the income left after the ones before, so they never double-count.
  • A new private deal: calls, coupon and exit (simplified, without ranges).
  • Capital calls you still owe on your current deals, which the Retirement Simulator leaves out.

The demo profile is a fictional sample household. Federal tax only. Not tax or financial advice.
